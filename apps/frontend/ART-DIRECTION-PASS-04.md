# XpoMag Art Direction Pass 04 — Pages 25–34

## Chapter direction
Food + hospitality now shifts away from the portrait-heavy language of the previous chapter. The main visual ideas are large venue imagery, image/copy splits, full-bleed atmosphere, dense utility content, and commercial pages that behave like real campaigns.

## Whitespace rule
Whitespace is intentional only when it creates hierarchy, pause, luxury, or focus. Otherwise the composition should be occupied by useful copy, image, metadata, navigation, or editorial devices.

## Reusable primitives introduced
- `hospitalityEditorialPage(...)` — large 52/48 image/copy split with body columns, metadata grid and pull quote.
- `fullBleedVenuePage(...)` — full-page photography with controlled gradient and editorial copy overlay.
- `fashionAdvertPage(...)` — reusable portrait-led full-page campaign template.
