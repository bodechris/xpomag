# XpoMag Art Direction Refinement 09

This pass is a publication-wide cleanup focused on the user review screenshots.

- Cover right-side story copy is white for legibility over the portrait.
- Rosebank 06:42 uses a full-page visual treatment rather than a cropped edge portrait.
- Pulse imagery is larger to reduce dead space while preserving the lower information modules.
- Rosebank by the Numbers is rebuilt as one full-page data composition so the title cannot be clipped by a narrow rail.
- Rosebank/Sandton city essays use materially larger portrait fields.
- Cover-story and sponsored-story display headlines use safer responsive sizes; imagery occupies more of each page.
- People pages use a non-overlapping 2x2 editorial grid.
- Working Day is rebalanced around a full-height left image with timeline content on the right.
- Meeting Guide reserves a dedicated header zone above the utility cards; body copy is more readable.
- Commercial portrait pages use larger image fields.
- Culture feature portraits and Keyes imagery are enlarged to fill otherwise dead areas.
- Studio Nine receives a larger, vertically dominant image.
- Hospitality and Rosebank Sunday Market imagery now use high-resolution Unsplash sources rather than the previous low-resolution assets.
- Headline sizing was tightened in high-risk layouts to reduce clipping throughout the issue.

Whitespace remains intentional only when it contributes hierarchy, luxury, tension or pacing.
