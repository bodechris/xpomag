# Page Flip Navigation — Pass 15

All navigation paths now use the magazine's physical page-turn transition instead of teleporting to a target spread.

- Cover/article story links animate toward their target spread, then update the URL/hash.
- Previous and Next already used the page-turn motion and continue to do so.
- Progress dots below the magazine now animate to their target spread instead of jumping instantly.
- Direct jumps choose direction from the target position and use the same flip sheet, fold shadow and highlight system.
- Interactive controls still retain their own click behavior.
